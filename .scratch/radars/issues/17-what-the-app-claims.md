# 17 — What is the app allowed to say about its coverage and its freshness, and how does it stay true?

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 02, 03, 16

## Question

What is the app allowed to say about its coverage and its freshness, and how does it stay true?

The dataset is one contractor's programme as of 01.09.2026. It is not every
enforcement point in Montenegro — there are legacy fixed cameras and mobile
patrols it knows nothing about.

- Does the app state that on its face, and how does it tell the driver that
  silence is not an all-clear?
- Blocking one-time acknowledgement, or passive footer? Which language governs
  the legally meaningful text?
- What does it promise about freshness — a named owner and trigger for
  re-extraction, a hard staleness warning past some age, or an explicit
  disclaimer of any promise?
- Is the extraction pipeline a committed, re-runnable deliverable or a
  throwaway agent run? The answer changes whether a refresh is a morning or a
  month.
- Is there a zero-infrastructure correction route (a mailto, a repo issue link)
  that does not reopen the no-backend decision?
- What does a returning offline user see once WebKit has evicted the precached
  bundle?
