Type: prototype

## Question

What does the warning sound like, and how does it behave against music, navigation and a call?

Chime or spoken phrase? If spoken, in which languages, recorded or synthesised,
under what licence? How long, how loud?

Then the part that decides whether it works at all: in a moving car with music
playing, a navigation prompt firing, or a Bluetooth call active — does the alert
duck the other audio, mix under it, or interrupt it? Get to something audible
and react to it rather than specifying it on paper.
