# 13 — What does an armed Drive survive, and does it ever end by itself?

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 11, 12

## Question

What does an armed Drive survive, and does it ever end by itself?

An incoming call, a screen lock, backgrounding, tab eviction, a reload. For
each: does the app fail loud and demand a re-arm at speed, silently re-acquire,
or hold continuous audio focus — a silent keepalive that ducks the driver's
music and navigation for hours?

And the one nobody specifies: does a Drive ever end on its own, or does it run
`watchPosition` and a wake lock until the battery is flat?
