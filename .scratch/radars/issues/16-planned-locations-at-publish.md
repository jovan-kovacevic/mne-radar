## Question

Do the planned Locations ship as precise points, degraded to city level, or not at all?

Depends on what `ZAVRSENO` turns out to mean, and on the legal position.

If they ship, the app publishes the coordinates of enforcement points that do
not yet exist. If they are withheld, does the `includePlanned` setting
disappear from the UI entirely, and does the map still show that something is
coming?
