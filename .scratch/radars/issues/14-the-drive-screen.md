# 14 — Is there a dedicated drive screen, and what happens when several Locations alert at once?

Type: prototype
Status: open
Blocked by: 06, 09

## Question

Is there a dedicated drive screen, and what happens when several Locations alert at once?

Does arming switch to a low-interaction screen — large type, banner dominant,
detail sheets and settings unreachable, fully functional with zero cached tiles
— or is the same Leaflet map the drive screen with a banner over it?

In central Podgorica the driver is almost always near something. When two or
more Locations alert within seconds, does the app coalesce to the nearest,
queue them serially, or announce a count?

Build it rough and look at it in a car.
