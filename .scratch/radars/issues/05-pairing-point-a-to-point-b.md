## Question

Does the source mechanically pair each POINT A with its POINT B, or must all 26 pairings be made by hand?

Without a derivable pairing there is no section length, no membership test, and
no "you are inside a measured section" — so the section-modelling decision is
unbuildable whichever way it is taken.

The evidence cuts both ways and a naive rule mis-pairs silently:

- 016/017 are a genuine consecutive A/B pair on M-10 Podgorica–Cetinje.
- 040 (POINT B, KOTOR) and 041 (POINT A, BUDVA) are consecutive IDs belonging
  to different sections in different cities.
- 044 and 046 are both POINT A.

One report claims every POINT A is followed by its POINT B at the next
consecutive ID, in the same municipality, 690 m–2221 m away. That is an
inference from a sample and must be checked against all 88 before anything
depends on it. A mis-pair produces a phantom 2 km enforced corridor across the
wrong road, and none of the planned validators would catch it.

Resolve with: whether a mechanical rule exists, its exact statement, and the
count of Locations it fails on.
